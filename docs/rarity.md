## Method

Statistical rarity used at generation time (and refreshed here from final metadata):

1. Count every trait value across all 8,888 tokens.
2. For each **generative** token, score = product of `(count / 8888)` for each scored trait.
3. **Lower score = rarer.** Rank 1 is the rarest generative piece.
4. Traits excluded from the score: `Rarity`, `Supply`, `Special`, `Name`, `Display Name`, `Community Handle`.
5. **Named specials** and **community 1-of-1s** are not ranked against the generative curve; they are **1 of 1**.
