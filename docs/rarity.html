Rarity — public on purpose

Every rank for all 8,888 WizNerdZ, published in full.

Publishing ranks costs buyers nothing here. Every sealed box of a tier is byte-identical and its contents were Merkle-committed before sale — so knowing exactly which wizards are rare cannot help anyone pick, refuse, or re-roll a box. Fairness comes from the commitment, not from secrecy. Projects that hide ranks create insiders; we'd rather you check our math.
